High School Competition

A dedicated track for secondary school students to explore statistics through creative work.

About the Track

This track introduces high school students to data storytelling and analytical thinking using official statistics published by NISR. Students work in small teams under a teacher's guidance to produce a creative data project.

What You Will Do

1

Learn

Get introduced to Rwanda's official statistics and how they are used.

2

Explore

Pick a topic and analyze the data behind it.

3

Create

Design a poster, infographic, or short presentation.

4

Present

Share findings with judges and peers.

Who Can Participate

  • Students enrolled in Rwandan secondary schools.
  • Teams formed per school guidelines (announced each edition).
  • Mentorship from a teacher is required.

Format

The High School track follows a simplified submission and evaluation process designed for younger participants. Full details — including submission format, deadlines, and prizes — are announced per edition.
