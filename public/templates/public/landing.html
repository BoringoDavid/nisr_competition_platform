Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

Rwandan citizens and students. Teams must have 2 members, with at least one Rwandan citizen.

How do I submit my work?

After registering, create a team, invite your teammate, then use the submission form on your dashboard.

Can I join more than one track?

Each team competes in one track per edition.

Is there a prize?

Yes — details are announced per edition on the NISR website and competition pages.
